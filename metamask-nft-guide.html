YOUR FIRST MINT: A METAMASK GUIDE

Temple Guide · Sep 3, 2026 · 6 min read · by Kaiser

A collector emailed us in August with a relic in his cart and a question he was embarrassed to ask: what exactly happens when I click mint, and how do I not mess it up? He'd been hovering over the button for 2 days. This is the walkthrough we sent back.

INSTALL METAMASK FROM THE REAL SITE

MetaMask is the standard Ethereum wallet, a browser extension that holds your keys and signs your transactions. Type metamask.io by hand. Fake wallet extensions buy search ads against the real one, and installing a fake is the one mistake nothing can undo. Setup gives you a 12 word recovery phrase. Write it on paper, keep the paper offline. Anyone who has those 12 words has the wallet.

MOVE SOME ETH IN

Buy ETH on an exchange like Coinbase or Kraken, then withdraw it to your MetaMask address. Send a small test amount first, 5 or 10 dollars, and confirm it arrives before you send the rest. Budget the NFT's price plus a cushion for gas, the network fee, which drops late at night and on weekends.

CONNECT AND VERIFY BEFORE YOU SIGN

On OpenSea or a collection site, Connect Wallet opens a MetaMask popup. Connecting is read-only and harmless. Signing moves money. So before any signature, check that the URL is one you typed or bookmarked yourself. Links from DMs are how most wallet thefts start, and the friendly ones do the most damage.

THE MINT ITSELF

Click mint and MetaMask shows the item price, the gas estimate, and the total. Read that screen slowly. It's the last point where a mistake costs nothing. Confirm, wait somewhere between seconds and a few minutes, and the NFT appears under MetaMask's NFT tab and on your marketplace profile.

Three habits cover almost everything: the recovery phrase never leaves the paper, URLs get typed or bookmarked instead of clicked from messages, and every confirmation screen gets read before it gets confirmed. Our collector minted the next morning and sent a one line email after: cannot believe I was scared of that.
