FAQ

1. What are NFTs?
An NFT (non-fungible token) is a unique digital asset on the blockchain. It confirms ownership of a one-of-a-kind item. In our case, a god.
2. What makes the CLAUDIUS collection unique?
Every relic is made in collaboration with Claude, in the pixel format that made doge famous, and crowned with ancient gold.
3. How can I buy a relic from this collection?
Relics can be purchased on the Collections page. Connect your wallet, choose your god, and mint.
4. What are the benefits of owning a CLAUDIUS relic?
Holders get future drops at cost, early lore chapters on the blog, and a vote on which god joins the pantheon next.
5. How can I contact you if I have other questions?
Email us or find us on X. Both links are in the footer below.
6. Which chain does CLAUDIUS live on?
Ethereum mainnet, as ERC-721 tokens. Artwork and metadata are pinned to IPFS, so your relic outlives any single server, including ours.
7. Are there royalties?
A 5% creator royalty on secondary sales pays for future drops, the Pantheon Vote, and the weekly lore scrolls.
