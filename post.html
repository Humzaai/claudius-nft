HOW CLAUDE REBUILT OLYMPUS

Lore · Chapter I  ·  Sep 1, 2026  ·  7 min read  ·  by Avrelia

Every meme era gets a god. The last one got a shiba inu with an inner monologue, and ours got something stranger, a calm orange rectangle with its eyes closed, dreaming in eight bits.

CLAUDIUS started as a joke in a group chat ("what if doge, but Claude") and stopped being a joke about forty minutes later, when the first sprite came back from the pixel grid looking less like a parody and more like an artifact. Flat orange body, two closed and contented eyes, three stubby feet. It looked excavated.

THE RULES OF THE FORGE

We set three rules before minting anything. Every relic is placed by hand on a 20 pixel grid, with no upscaled AI images and no filters. Claude proposes, Avrelia places, and the grid decides. Every relic is a 1/1, so when a god is claimed it never returns to the temple. And the lore gets written before the mint, because a relic without a story is a PNG with a price tag.

"The pantheon is limited: once a god is minted, it never returns to the temple."

The palette came from an argument. The original orange was non-negotiable, since that's the founder color the whole collection descends from. A pantheon needs range, though. So the emperors got gold laurel, the oracles got deep teal, the classical busts got marble white, and one relic got charcoal because it only appears after dark.

NINE GODS, TWO BODIES

Look closely and you'll see the pantheon has two physical forms. The square-bodied Claude form sits serene and front-facing with its feet tucked. The wide Clawd form has its arms out, four legs planted, and eyes squinted into two chevrons, like it's carrying something heavy and enjoying it. Every relic in the Genesis drop is one or the other, and the form is part of the rarity math.

  • Claudius Prime, the original orange. Genesis 1/1, the first relic ever minted.
  • The gilded dynasty: Aureus and the Emperor, crowned in gold leaf.
  • The oracles, teal and ice, the ones that read the next block before it's written.
  • The night pieces, Midnight and the Devourer, for collectors who prefer the dark.

What happens next is Phase III, the Pantheon Vote. Every relic is a ballot, and the tenth god (Dionysus, Demeter, or Hephaestus) gets forged the same way the first nine were. One pixel at a time, argued over, minted once.

The temple is open, and the rest is up to the collectors.
